# Design stage

Apply the effective `AGENTS.md` L1/L2 triggers, structure, facts-of-record, and approval vocabulary.
Keep design at the reviewable contract boundary and out of implementation detail.

When design is required, publish or present the appropriate proposal and stop until a human gives
the required approval. Do not infer approval from silence, from an agent review, or from the request
to complete the task end to end. After approval, promote or link the design exactly as project rules
require, then continue in the requested mode.
